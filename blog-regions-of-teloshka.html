A Spoiler-Light Guide to the Political Regions of Teloshka

By P. S. Davis · Posted June 2026 · 11 minute read

Teloshka is divided by geography, faith, history, trade, and old blood. The West and the East do not organise themselves in quite the same way, which can make the politics difficult to follow at first.

This guide is intended to help readers understand the major regions, cities, seats of power, and public representatives without revealing hidden loyalties, deaths, betrayals, or later political turns. It describes public structures and broad associations, not every secret at work beneath them.

A note on the West

Historically, Aurenvia and Tollitch were treated as separate western regions. By the time of the main Essence Wars sequence, they are generally understood together as Aurenvia Tollitch. This political identity does not fully encompass the North, Zerthys, or Roydne, though each sits within the wider western political orbit in different ways. Roydne remains one of the newest and most difficult western establishments to recognise formally.

The West

The West is politically centred on Aurenvia Tollitch, but its wider orbit includes newer settlements, contested borderlands, the independent-minded North, and the Kingdom of Zerthys.

Aurenvia Tollitch

Political capital: Haithe

Primary political seat: Castle of Haithe

Political body: The Order of Aurenvia Tollitch

Known public role: Largest western political establishment in Teloshka

Aurenvia Tollitch is the great western political power, formed from the historical union of Aurenvia and Tollitch. It is not a single city-state, but a vast political structure of regions, castles, towns, coastal powers, religious centres, and old alliances. The Order of Aurenvia Tollitch meets in Haithe and serves as the governing council of the western lands.

Haithe

Region: Aurenvia

Primary seat: Castle of Haithe

Known political importance: Political capital of Aurenvia Tollitch

Known associated figures: Lord Paramount Chalm of Haithe, Dharl Bodwin, Marcius Saylong

Haithe is the political heart of the West. Its castle, great hall, Central Quarters, and noble residences place it at the centre of Order politics. It is the place where the West’s wider decisions are argued, delayed, sharpened, and finally acted upon.

Keis, Hayn’s Town, and Mermard’s Town

Region: Tollitch

Primary seat: Keis’s Castle

Known political association: Protected under Keis

Known representative: Brigetta Keis

Keis, Hayn’s Town, and Mermard’s Town function as a united region under the protection and influence of Keis. Keis’s Castle is one of the great western strongholds, with military and political weight far beyond its walls.

Hawn’s Keep

Region: Aurenvia Tollitch

Primary seat: Hawn’s Keep

Known representative: Xania of Hawn’s Keep

Hawn’s Keep is a separate western entity with its own political weight. Its power is less about territory alone and more about influence, intelligence, wealth, and careful positioning within the Order.

Armekalia

Region: Aurenvia Tollitch

Primary seat: Armekalia

Known representative: Mortas Lexx

Known character: Two large coastal cities with a fierce naval tradition, represented by commanders such as Lewisear Lexx and Halicern warships such as The Sorthalix

Armekalia is one of the strongest maritime powers in the West. It is famed for its navy, its coastal strength, and its willingness to act where other regions debate. Its representatives carry the interests of Armekalia into the Order, though its true power is often felt at sea.

Talill

Region: Aurenvia Tollitch

Primary seat: Talill

Known representative: Erastus of Talill

Known character: Intelligence, secrecy, old networks

Talill is most publicly associated with Erastus and his network of information. Its role in the Order is less visibly military and more strategic, relying on spies, whispers, private channels, and knowledge gathered from places others cannot reach.

Lenne

Region: Aurenvia Tollitch

Primary towns and lands: Lenne, Lucarion, Cassarish, Brayre’s Farm

Known representative: Lady Vera of Lenne

Known associated figures: Allturna Vehzar, Merjhan Vehzar

Lenne is a large southern region of the Order, with Lucarion, Cassarish, and Brayre’s Farm falling within its wider sphere. It provides a substantial southern presence within Aurenvia Tollitch and is closely tied to religious conviction, political ambition, and regional pride.

Lucarion

Region: Lenne, Aurenvia Tollitch

Known political building: The Copper Hall

Known associated figure: Governor Dubaris

Lucarion is one of the key southern cities of the West. Its Copper Hall stands as a major civic and political building, while its position near Roydne and the southern coast gives it strategic importance.

Roydne

Region: Southwestern coast

Capital: Roydne City

Known representatives: Sherptarl of Roydne and Ganflide of Roydne

Known character: Trade, coastal wealth, piracy, rebellion, and uneasy recognition

Roydne is one of the newest western establishments tied to the Order. Its wealth comes from trade, seafaring, and coastal commerce, but its politics have long been complicated by piracy, loose governance, and resentment toward Aurenvia Tollitch. The Roydne-Aurenvia Tollitch Pact was intended to limit pirate activity and draw Roydne more firmly into the western political order, yet many within the region remain divided over what that loyalty should mean. Roydne City, its capital, is the southernmost known city in Teloshka.

Citadel of Jemyne

Region: Western lands

Primary seat: Citadel of Jemyne

Known representative: Uterra

Known character: Law, order, restraint

The Citadel of Jemyne is a western political and legal centre associated with strict order. Its voice within the Order tends toward caution, stability, and mediation rather than zeal or conquest.

Jolda, Skaldrith, and Skogfell

Region: Western woodland territories

Primary seat: Jolda

Associated settlements: Skaldrith and Skogfell

Known representative: Lady Gray of Jolda

Jolda is a city entwined with the forest and protected by natural defences. It covers or politically shelters Skaldrith and Skogfell, giving it a broad woodland identity. Its rulers once sought sovereignty, but Jolda ultimately came under the wider protection of Aurenvia Tollitch.

Alrece Town

Region: Aurenvia Tollitch

Primary seat: Alrece Town

Known representative: Hugo of Alrece

Known character: Religious influence and local western identity

Alrece Town is a western settlement with a voice in the Order through Hugo of Alrece. Its public identity is strongly tied to faith and regional representation, rather than direct military command.

Orynbeck

Region: Northern Aurenvia Tollitch

Primary seat: Orynbeck

Known representative: Paldine

Known associated figure: Kaedryn Harth by upbringing

Orynbeck is a quiet northern village within Aurenvia Tollitch. It is not one of the great centres of power, but it matters through its local leadership, its place in the Order, and its connection to lives shaped far from the grand chambers of Haithe.

The North

Region: North of the Madita River and surrounding tribal lands

Known representative: Ramcino

Known associated figures: Deyra Fjornskara, Troraim, Rysna Rawno

Known character: Tribal strength, old rivalries, fierce independence

The North is not simply another part of Aurenvia Tollitch. It is a collection of tribes, villages, rivalries, and alliances with its own identity. Some northern figures seek recognition within the wider western structure, though the North remains proud, difficult to govern, and deeply shaped by memory.

Kingdom of Zerthys

Capital: Zerthys

Known ruler: King Yuspe

Known representative: Oran

Known character: Vast northern kingdom, warrior culture, old treaty ties

Zerthys is a major western-aligned kingdom, though it should not be treated as simply another Aurenvian or Tollitchian district. It is linked to the West through treaty, history, and political representation, while retaining its own monarchy and identity.

The East

The Eastern Union is not organised like Aurenvia Tollitch. Its regions are larger, older, and more formally federated, with power shared through the Council of the Eastern Accord.

The Eastern Union

Political centre: Gusia City

Primary political body: Council of the Eastern Accord

Primary council building: Gusia City’s Accord Chamber

Known character: Federation-like union of large eastern regions

The Eastern Union is not organised like Aurenvia Tollitch. Its regions are larger, older, and more formally federated. Power is shared through the Council of the Eastern Accord, though not always equally. The East is bound by Yesdelism, trade, military coordination, and shared fear of western aggression, yet each region guards its own interests.

The Gainfolds

Capital: Gusia City

Political seat: Accord Chamber, Gusia City

Major landmark: The Eastern Spire

Known representative: Arlen Velthar, High Steward of the Accord

Known character: Political heart of the Eastern Union

The Gainfolds form the administrative and political centre of the Eastern Union. Gusia City is one of the largest and most influential cities in Teloshka, home to the Council of the Eastern Accord and the institutions that hold the Union together. Its wealth, scale, and central position make it powerful, though that dominance also breeds resentment.

Fone

Capital: Khartrim

Known representative: Sylvara Cathen, Marshal of the Greenhold

Known military force: Knights of the Greenhold

Known character: Defensive strength, agriculture, road control, and protection of the Union’s inner routes

Fone is a major eastern region whose importance lies in defence, food, roads, and military discipline. Its Knights of the Greenhold are famous for guarding the critical routes between Elanwyn and the rest of the Eastern Union, especially the passages that once protected access to the Herastium Fields.

Kingdom of Elanwyn

Capital: Domen

Royal palace: Palace of Krio

Royal seat: The Herastium Throne

Known ruler: King Hart II

Known representative to the Accord: Lady Elyth Draemir, Envoy of the Northmarch

Major city: Rolinsiamon

Known character: Vast northern kingdom, Herastium wealth, contested power

Elanwyn is a massive northern kingdom within the Eastern Union’s political world. Domen is its capital and the seat of the Palace of Krio, while Rolinsiamon stands as one of the great northern cities. Elanwyn’s importance is inseparable from the Herastium Fields and the rare materials they produce. Its distance from the southern heart of the Union gives it both value and vulnerability.

Northmarch

Region: Elanwyn

Known representative: Lady Elyth Draemir

Known character: Northern power base within Elanwyn

Northmarch is not the whole of Elanwyn, but it is one of its most important internal powers. Its influence is military, noble, and political, giving Lady Elyth Draemir a strong public position beyond her role at the Eastern Accord.

Realm

Capital: Devantondie

Known representative: Lord Krasmay D’Velthon, Warden of the Falcon Watch

Known character: Island nation, naval strength, coastal defence

Realm is the only island nation within the Eastern Union’s political structure. Its capital, Devantondie, is a major eastern city, and its position gives Realm a distinct naval identity. It guards its autonomy carefully and often views mainland politics through the lens of security, sea power, and survival.

Verdathisa

Major city: Jonika

Major military site: Garette Fort

Major waters: Ihrlime Sea

Known representative: Maedras Ilvare, Warden of the Southern Reach

Known civic seat: Jonikan Regal Courthouse

Known civic figures: Sheriff Edrik Fenthos, Lieutenant Caslyn Armond, Captain Myral Corthain

Known character: Food production, southern defence, river and sea trade

Verdathisa is famed for food production, fertile lands, and its relationship with the sparkling waters of the Ihrlime Sea. Jonika is one of its most important towns, while Garette Fort is a vital military site. Verdathisa often sits where trade, food, defence, and war collide.

Lirioneth

Capital: Citadel of Aliztar

Major city: Luza

Political body: The Silver Assembly

Known political building: Hall of Arms, Citadel of Aliztar

Known ruler or power-holder: Chancellor Caldyr Rhaelmar

Known representative to the Accord: Karad Torveth, Chancellor’s Emissary

Known military figures: General Thalrice Arondar, Edran Valtir, Captain of the Shadow Guard

Known character: Largest protected eastern landmass, military strength, Yesdelist scholarship

Lirioneth is one of the most protected and strategically important lands in the East. By land mass alone, it is the largest of the eastern mainland regions, protected by the Ihrlime Sea to the west and harsh desert to the east. The Citadel of Aliztar serves as its capital, military centre, and symbolic heart. The Silver Assembly operates from the Hall of Arms, and the Chancellor’s influence reaches far beyond Lirioneth’s borders.

Strategic sites

Some places in Teloshka matter less as ordinary settlements and more as military routes, resource zones, sacred locations, or waters that shape the movement of armies and trade.

Garette Fort

Region: Verdathisa

Type: Military fortification

Importance: Southern shield, contested military site, and one of the key flashpoints between East and West

Herastium Fields

Region: Kingdom of Elanwyn

Type: Strategic resource zone

Importance: Source of Herastium Plocedies and Plocetol, among the most valuable resources in Teloshka

Union Pass

Region: Near Fone and Elanwyn’s southern approaches

Type: Military route and defensive choke point

Importance: Historically guarded against western incursion and linked to the protection of the Herastium Fields

Island of Gor

Region: Island beyond ordinary mainland politics

Type: Sacred and secretive island

Importance: Associated with Kherastite, the Kherastis Tree, and hidden religious-political forces

Sea of Thewthyri

Type: Major western and southern sea

Importance: Divides and connects western and eastern operations, trade routes, naval patrols, and invasions

Ihrlime Sea

Type: Major eastern sea

Importance: Central to Verdathisa, Lirioneth, Jonika, Garette Fort, Rolinsiamon, and the eastern coastal world
