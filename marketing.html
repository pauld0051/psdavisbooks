Marketing & Collaboration

Why this page exists

There are a lot of scams targeting authors. To protect readers, my work, and my time, I use a single, safe intake process. If you have a genuine opportunity, please read the guidelines below and submit via the official form. I will review and respond if it is a strong fit.

Before you pitch

Common requests I receive include: general marketing packages, paid reviews, book club placements, trailer creation, social media management, PR blasts, influencer posts, and pay-to-enter awards. Most of these are not needed. Consider carefully whether your offer is specific, useful, and relevant to my current needs.

Submission rules

  • All opportunities must be submitted via the form below. No exceptions, even for free offers.
  • No payments will be made before I have assessed viability and value.
  • Provide clear deliverables, audience details, dates, and any refund or cancellation terms.
  • Include verifiable references or case studies and links I can check.
  • No pressure tactics or limited-time demands. Respectful communication only.
  • Official contact is via email at p.s.davis@psdavisbooks.com.

Hardline anti-scam policy

I will not engage with unsolicited promotions that bypass this page or the form. Any request that asks for payment without clear, verifiable value will be declined.
