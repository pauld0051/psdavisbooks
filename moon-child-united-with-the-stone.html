Taemi Lor Equillis was born for the council.

Now of age, she’s ready to step into leadership in the kingdom of Adrienan—but dreams instead of the world beyond its walls. When her father gives her a magical family pendant, she learns she may be destined for far more than diplomacy.

Pursued by those who seek the pendant's power, Taemi flees with a young soldier to the hidden elven city in the Laquar Forest. There, she begins to unravel the truth of her heritage, her magic, and her purpose as a descendant of the moon.

Moon Child: United With the Stone is a YA fantasy of legacy, danger, and self-discovery.
