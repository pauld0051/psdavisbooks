The Essence Wars continues

The Anvil and the Throne arrives September 30

Release date: September 30, 2026

Peace held Teloshka together for nearly eighty years. It will not survive what comes next.

The truth behind Garette Fort leads north to Elanwyn, where a failing king sits upon a throne others have already begun to divide. Maerwyn Sawngfli has one chance to reach Domen before the forces gathering around it close their grip.

Kaedryn Harth and Grantchu Chermoine carry a truth that could stop a war neither side understands, but returning home may mean execution. Across Teloshka, armies move towards the Herastium Fields, assassins pass through royal halls, and the gifted are turned into weapons.

Every road leads deeper into war.

eARCs of The Anvil and the Throne are now available by request. These are digital ebook copies for readers who would like to read before release and, if they choose, leave an honest review.

I also recommend reading The White Beast of Trie, a Kindle Unlimited exclusive, before opening Book Two. It is a short detour into the Trie Range, but it plants something that will make one moment in The Anvil and the Throne land much harder. You will know it when you reach it.
