An Envious God is now available

An Envious God has officially launched. This second book in The Essence Wars is set six years after The Seeker’s Wrath and returns to a world strained by broken treaties, vanishing gifted, and quiet acts of betrayal. Maerwyn Sawngfli and her warhound Braegor are drawn into the aftermath of the Massacre at Garette Fort, where the truth behind a ruined stronghold may unmake the Eastern Union itself.

Thank you to everyone who has pre ordered, shared posts, or already picked up a copy. Your support keeps this series alive and growing.

If you enjoy the book, one of the most helpful things you can do is leave a short review. Even a few lines on a retailer site or Goodreads helps new readers decide to take a chance on the story.
