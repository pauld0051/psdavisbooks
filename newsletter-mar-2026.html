The White Beast of Trie is out now

The White Beast of Trie is now available on Amazon, free to read with Kindle Unlimited or to buy for $0.99.

Set high in the Trie Range, this short story follows Brother Arley, a quiet man of ledgers, prayer, and routine, after a string of violent deaths shatters the peace of his monastery. Forced into a hard country with a man he does not trust, Arley is drawn into a hunt shaped by fear, faith, and a creature no one seems willing to understand.

To save it may cost him everything. To surrender it may cost far more.

If you enjoy the world of The Essence Wars, or simply want a darker, tighter story that can be read in a sitting or two, this is a strong place to step into the wider series.

The next book is nearing release

The Anvil and the Throne, the next main entry in The Essence Wars, is now undergoing its final touches.

The current target release date is June 30. That would place it one year after the release of The Seeker’s Wrath, even though the story itself takes place six years later. It is still a tentative date, but that is the mark I am aiming for.

More details, previews, and release news will follow as the book moves into the final stretch.

If you have read The Seeker’s Wrath, An Envious God, or both, please consider leaving a review. Reviews make a real difference. They help readers decide whether to take a chance on a series, and they help independent authors far more than most people realise.
