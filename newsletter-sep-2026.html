Today, Book Two of The Essence Wars belongs to its readers.

This book has travelled a long road to reach you. Thank you to everyone who read an early copy, shared a post, recommended the series, left a review, sent a message, or simply stayed with me while Teloshka continued to grow.

Independent books do not travel far on their own. Every message, recommendation and quiet act of support helped carry The Anvil and the Throne to release day. I am enormously grateful.
