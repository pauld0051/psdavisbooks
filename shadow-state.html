By J. D. Mercer.

Shadow State: How Private Contractors Control The US Government is a hard-hitting exposé on one of the least understood threats to democracy: the rise of private intelligence firms, defense contractors, and corporate mercenaries who operate beyond government oversight.

From covert wars and black-budget operations to AI-driven psychological warfare and autonomous assassination programs, this book reveals how unelected private entities hold more power over national security, foreign policy, and even domestic governance than elected officials.

Inside these pages, you’ll discover:

  • The secretive world of defense giants like Lockheed Martin, Raytheon, and Northrop Grumman—and how they dictate U.S. military actions.
  • How firms like Palantir, Booz Allen Hamilton, and NSO Group run global surveillance networks beyond public scrutiny.
  • The truth about private military contractors (PMCs) replacing soldiers in Iraq, Afghanistan, and even domestic law enforcement.
  • How AI-driven cognitive warfare is used to manipulate elections, protests, and public perception—without people realizing it.
  • The hidden research bases, black-site programs, and synthetic consciousness experiments happening beyond Area 51.
  • How whistleblowers and journalists who get too close often disappear—permanently.
  • What happens next if this system remains unchecked—and whether accountability is still possible.

This is not speculation. The infrastructure for total corporate control of war, intelligence, and even human thought is already being built.

If you don’t know who really runs the government—it’s time to find out.
